## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a high-trust, premium collaborative parcel transport bridge between France and Benin. Combining the structural dependability of European institutional fintech with the warm, dynamic hospitality of West African trade corridors, the UI prioritizes peer-to-peer security, transparent verification, and seamless travel logistics.

The visual direction blends **Corporate / Modern** fintech precision with soft **Tactile & Pillowed** ergonomics. Interfaces rely on pure white elevated cards anchored against warm ivory and slate undertones, punctuated by logistic orange propulsion and verified emerald accents. It avoids cold administrative rigidity in favor of an inviting, human-centered escort experience where every handoff, tracking checkpoint, and kilo allowance feels tactile, verified, and effortless.

## Colors

The palette grounds high-value cross-border logistics in authoritative slate tones while signaling speed, warmth, and validation through deliberate accent hits:

- **Primary (`#0F172A`)**: Deep Navy Slate establishes foundational gravity, security guarantees, key headline anchors, and primary interactive buttons.
- **Secondary (`#FF6B35`)**: Energetic Logistic Orange represents movement, transit triggers, call-to-action highlights, and real-time cargo updates across international routes.
- **Tertiary (`#10B981`)**: Benin Emerald Green serves as the trust layer—identifying verified passports, secure escrow releases, completed baggage handoffs, and positive traveler reputation.
- **Neutral (`#64748B`)**: Slate Gray governs supporting meta-data, flight numbers, input borders, and tertiary icon treatments.
- **Backgrounds & Canvas**: Surfaces transition between subtle warm ivory canvas tints (`#FDFBF7`), clean slate off-whites (`#F8FAFC`), and stark pure white (`#FFFFFF`) card containers to deliver crisp depth without visual exhaustion.

## Typography

The typography pairs **Plus Jakarta Sans** for structural headers with **Inter** for dense, legible transactional copy. Plus Jakarta Sans brings modern, friendly geometry to city pairs (e.g., Paris CDG ⇄ Cotonou COO), rate cards, and profile headings. Inter provides clear, neutral legibility for passport verification tables, price per kilogram calculations, dimensions, and custom declaration summaries. 

Numeric details—such as available baggage allowances, dates, and currency (EUR/XOF)—maintain tabular, proportional spacing for immediate scanning during travel validation.

## Layout & Spacing

This design system uses a balanced 12-column grid on desktop screens down to a 4-column fluid layout for mobile devices. 

- **Desktop (1024px+)**: Max container width of `1200px` centered with `2rem` page margins and `1.5rem` gutters. Search, flight matching, and tracking listings utilize asymmetric split views (e.g., 7 columns for trip listings, 5 columns for interactive route & escrow previews).
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px)**: 8-column grid with `1.5rem` outer margins. Filter controls collapse into top horizontal scrollbars or bottom sheets.
- **Mobile (below 768px)**: 4 columns with edge-to-edge `1rem` margins and `1rem` gutters. Primary booking cards and transit route steppers stack vertically with rapid touch-friendly padding.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses warm, diffuse ambient drop shadows alongside layered tonal surfaces to convey clear spatial separation:

- **Level 0 (Base Canvas)**: Neutral ivory-tinted slate background (`#FDFBF7` or `#F8FAFC`). Flat, grounding the interface.
- **Level 1 (Card & Module Layer)**: Pure white (`#FFFFFF`) containers resting with an ultra-soft diffuse drop shadow: `box-shadow: 0 4px 20px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.02)`. Outlined with a faint border (`rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06)`).
- **Level 2 (Active Trip Cards & Floating Controls)**: Elevated booking forms, transit trackers, and hovered items: `box-shadow: 0 12px 32px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals, Overlays & Verification Drawers)**: Verification photo flows, baggage inspection checklists, and safety guarantees: `box-shadow: 0 24px 48px -12px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.16)`.

## Shapes

The design system adopts a welcoming **pillowed geometry** (`roundedness: 3`) reflecting organic travel shapes, flight paths, and friendly peer-to-peer security. 

- Standard inputs, buttons, and badges leverage full-pill silhouettes (`9999px`) or ultra-rounded corner radiuses (`1rem` / `16px`).
- Major cards, modular itinerary wrappers, and identity verification modules feature generous rounded corners (`2rem` / `32px` to `3rem` / `48px`).
- Avatars, luggage kilo tokens, and flight status chips remain strictly circular or pill-shaped to reinforce a soft, tactile, high-trust consumer fintech aesthetic.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action**: Deep Navy Slate (`#0F172A`) fill with pure white label and subtle hover lift. Fully pill-shaped (`9999px`), 48px or 56px height, tracking-snug label.
- **Secondary / Transit Action**: Logistic Orange (`#FF6B35`) fill with pure white label, reserved for booking confirmations, flight publishing, and package claims.
- **Tertiary / Ghost**: Transparent base with hairline slate border (`rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.12)`) and navy text. Soft ivory background shift on hover.

### Flight & Package Cards
- White background (`#FFFFFF`) on rounded-3xl (`24px` to `32px`) borders.
- Header showcases the departure and arrival corridor (e.g., Paris ➔ Cotonou) with custom flight pill badges, traveler verification status (emerald checkmark with micro-pill badge), available weight quota (e.g., `12 kg restants`), and base unit price (`8€ / kg`).
- Footer embeds split actions with real-time identity trust score indicators.

### Micro-Badges & Status Chips
- **Verified Traveler**: Emerald Green tint background (`#ECFDF5`), bold emerald text (`#059669`), 12px pill geometry with a micro security checkmark.
- **Cargo In Transit**: Warm Orange tint background (`#FFF7ED`), deep orange text (`#C2410C`), with an active pulse indicator.
- **Customs Cleared**: Slate blue tint background (`#F1F5F9`), slate text (`#334155`).

### Input Fields & Search Bars
- Dual-origin travel selector: Origin / Destination / Date / Kilograms input assembled into an interconnected pill island.
- Inactive state utilizes subtle off-white fill (`#F8FAFC`) with soft slate borders. Active focus switches to a pure white field with a 2px Deep Navy Slate outline and diffused glow.

### Trust & Identity Indicators
- Biometric & passport verification cards featuring dual-tier check marks, escort track records, escrow payment hold notifications, and mutual identity guarantees presented in rounded, elevated pill-containers.
